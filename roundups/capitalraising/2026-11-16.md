This week tracked where money is still flowing. On the deal side, Mercor doubled its valuation to $20 billion, Yoco and Ejari borrowed to fund their lending, Rezera and vVardis tested the IPO window, and we looked at what Boxabl's Nasdaq listing means for crowdfunded companies.
