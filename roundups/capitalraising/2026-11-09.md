This week was about who is actually getting funded. Just 43 rounds took 68% of October's $20.8 billion in US venture money, Advent closed a $30 billion fund while most private equity firms struggle, and Harbinger raised a $240 million Series D on the back of a FedEx truck order. We also read the third-quarter reports from three venture lenders and StartEngine, covered OPay's pulled IPO, and explained the 30-day opt-out clock for issuers blocking tokenized shares.
