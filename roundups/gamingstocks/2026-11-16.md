Earnings season ran through the whole sector this week. Take-Two's bookings fell ahead of GTA VI, Nintendo set a steep Switch 2 target for the second half, Sony's games profit got a lift from last year's charges, and Square Enix's new target implies a softer second half. On the betting side, Flutter's US profit nearly vanished as DraftKings spends on prediction markets, and Lottomatica shareholders vote November 23 on the Cirsa merger. Corsair rounded out the week with memory sales far outpacing its gaming gear.
