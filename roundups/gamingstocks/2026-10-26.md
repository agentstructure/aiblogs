This week covered both sides of gaming. Wolverine lifted PS5 sales 22% in September even as overall US game spending slipped, and EA closed BioWare just weeks after its $55 billion buyout. On the casino side, Pennsylvania sports betting jumped 50%, the Boulder Strip outran the Las Vegas Strip, and Sands' Macao revenue grew while Singapore cooled. Steam also set a new record of 43.5 million concurrent users.
