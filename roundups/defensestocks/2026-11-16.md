Budget politics and backlogs shared the week. Congress returned for a lame-duck session with defense funding set to run out December 11, and incoming Armed Services chair Adam Smith took aim at Iran war funds. Rocket Lab, Hensoldt and Applied Aerospace showed how big backlogs can sit beside weak cash flow or a softer outlook, and we compared the ITA, XAR and SHLD defense ETFs and previewed Elbit's report after a fresh rocket artillery order.
