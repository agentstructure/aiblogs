## What happened

Nexon (3659) reported third-quarter results on Nov. 10, and the headline numbers were strong. According to the company's [earnings release](https://www.gamespress.com/zh-TW/Nexon-Releases-Earnings-for-Third-Quarter-2026), revenue was ¥138.4 billion, up 17% from a year earlier, and operating income was ¥41.8 billion, up 11%. Net income was ¥38.1 billion, roughly flat. Nexon said revenue, operating income and net income all came in above its own outlook.

In won terms, [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/28395/nexon-q3-revenue-up-17-to-12-trillion-won) put revenue at about 1.23 trillion won and operating income at 371.8 billion won. Nine-month revenue was ¥411.7 billion, up 17%, and nine-month operating income was ¥131.2 billion, up 12%, according to [Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/123700/nexon-q3-operating-profit-3718-billion-won-sets-record-on-maplestory-fc-online). Nexon says its full-year revenue and operating income are on track for record highs.

The release also carries a caution that matters more than the beat. Currency flattered the quarter: Nexon reported revenue growth of 17% as reported but 9% in constant currency. Operating income was flat on a constant-currency basis, partly because the prior-year quarter included a one-time ¥3.9 billion gain from a trust liquidation. Net income was helped by a ¥7.5 billion fund valuation gain. Read through those items, this was a good quarter, not a spectacular one.

## One franchise did the heavy lifting

MapleStory revenue rose 72% year over year to an all-time quarterly high, per the release. The company's three largest franchises, MapleStory, Dungeon&Fighter and FC, together produced ¥108.5 billion, up 24%. Everything else, grouped as "other titles," produced ¥29.9 billion, down 4%.

The MapleStory detail is a platform story. Global MapleStory revenue doubled, with China nearly quadrupling, Taiwan doubling and the West up 33%, per the release. Korea's MapleStory revenue rose 19%, and the sandbox platform MapleStory Worlds roughly doubled. [Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/123700/nexon-q3-operating-profit-3718-billion-won-sets-record-on-maplestory-fc-online) attributes the overseas PC gain to classic servers in China and Taiwan and to collaboration content in North America and Europe, and the domestic PC gain to summer and autumn updates. In other words, the growth came from an old PC title that Nexon kept refreshing, not from a new console or mobile hit.

FC, Nexon's football franchise, returned to double-digit growth. The release says FC Online 2 posted its highest quarterly revenue, while FC Mobile traffic recovered sequentially and rose year over year. Nexon did not disclose absolute revenue for individual titles, so the size of each contribution cannot be checked.

## Where it did not work

Dungeon&Fighter revenue fell 33% year over year and came in below Nexon's outlook, with both the China PC and mobile versions declining, according to the release. The top-three group still grew 24%, which shows how much MapleStory covered for it.

The regional split in [Inven Global's coverage](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/28399/maplestory-drives-nexons-q3-revenue-surpasses-12-trillion-won) shows the same unevenness: Korea up 17%, North America and Europe up 41%, other regions up 77%, but China down 7% and Japan down 13%. China is the market where MapleStory nearly quadrupled and Dungeon&Fighter fell, so the single-market exposure cuts both ways.

Other titles were mixed. THE FINALS revenue declined slightly year over year, and Mabinogi grew sequentially but fell year over year. ARC Raiders, the PC and console shooter from Nexon's Embark Studios, has sold more than 17 million units to date, according to the release. Nexon said its revenue was within outlook, but gave no year-over-year figure. Nexon also said it has discontinued six projects in recent months and is moving budgets and teams to games with greater commercial potential.

## The Q4 outlook is the real signal

Nexon's fourth-quarter outlook, per the release:

- Revenue of ¥107.7 billion to ¥118.9 billion, which is 13% to 4% below the year-ago quarter as reported, and 20% to 12% below in constant currency.
- Operating income of ¥4.0 billion to ¥11.7 billion, against ¥41.8 billion in Q3. The release puts the year-over-year change at minus 44% to plus 63%, a range wide enough to show how much is still open.
- Net income of ¥4.7 billion to ¥10.6 billion, down 57% to 2%.

Nexon did not state the year-ago Q4 revenue in the release. Working backward from the percentages, the base is roughly ¥124 billion, which is this article's own arithmetic rather than a company figure. [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/28399/maplestory-drives-nexons-q3-revenue-surpasses-12-trillion-won) reports that the net income decline reflects a high base from ¥16.6 billion of currency and securities gains in Q4 2025, and that Nexon projects MapleStory growth of about 70% year over year in Q4.

If MapleStory is still growing around 70% and total revenue is still projected to fall, something large is shrinking or the prior-year quarter was unusually strong. The release does not say which, and the Q4 2025 comparison is the first thing to check when full figures are available.

The full-year outlook, per the release, is revenue of ¥519.4 billion to ¥530.6 billion, up 9% to 12%, and operating income of ¥135.2 billion to ¥142.9 billion, up 9% to 15%. Because nine-month results are already in, the full-year range simply restates the Q4 range, so it adds no new information.

## Why it matters and what to watch

For investors following platform trends, the quarter is a reminder that PC remains a profit engine for Asian publishers when a long-running title is paired with steady content updates. It is also a reminder that concentration is the cost: one franchise rose 72% while another fell 33%, and the combined figure depended on currency and one-time items.

Nexon is also returning cash. The release lists a special dividend of ¥415 per share, about ¥324 billion (roughly $2 billion), payable Nov. 25 to holders of record as of Sept. 30, plus a year-end dividend of ¥30 per share, which brings the regular annual dividend to ¥60.

Several items are worth tracking from here:

- **New launches.** [Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/123700/nexon-q3-operating-profit-3718-billion-won-sets-record-on-maplestory-fc-online) reports that Overgeared launches Nov. 26 in South Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau, and that Azur Promilia begins domestic service the same day. Dungeon & Fighter Raising, a mobile idle RPG, is planned for a global launch in the first half of 2027.
- **Dungeon&Fighter in China.** Whether the 33% decline stabilizes is the clearest test of the portfolio's balance.
- **Currency.** Nexon's own sensitivity table says a one-yen move against the dollar shifts Q4 revenue by ¥0.71 billion, against an 8-point gap between reported and constant-currency revenue growth in Q3.
- **Q4 results.** Whether Nexon lands inside its range will show if the lower fourth-quarter guide reflects timing or a weaker base business.
