## A week with no scheduled catalysts

If you opened an earnings calendar looking for gaming stocks reporting this week, you will mostly find nothing. The [TipRanks calendar for Thursday, Oct. 8](https://www.tipranks.com/calendars/earnings/2026-10-8) lists names such as PepsiCo (PEP), Tilray (TLRY) and Progressive (PGR), and none of the rows displayed is a video game publisher, casino operator or sportsbook. That page did not fully load for me and hinted at more listings than it showed, so treat it as a strong signal rather than a guarantee.

That is normal for early October. Quarter-end was Sept. 30, and the big gaming reporters cluster in the last week of October and the first half of November. The useful work this week is knowing the dates, because several of them carry unusual weight this cycle.

## The calendar that matters

Four release dates have been confirmed in company announcements:

- **Caesars Entertainment (CZR), Tuesday Oct. 27, after the close.** [The company said](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261002880215/en/Caesars-Entertainment-Inc.-to-Release-2026-Third-Quarter-Results-on-October-27-2026) it will not host an earnings call, citing its pending merger with Fertitta Entertainment.
- **Gaming and Leisure Properties (GLPI), Thursday Oct. 29, after the close,** with a [call on Friday Oct. 30 at 10:00 a.m. ET](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/GLPI/gaming-and-leisure-properties-inc-schedules-third-quarter-2026-91tj97l5obr7.html).
- **Accel Entertainment (ACEL), Tuesday Nov. 3, after the close,** with a [4:30 p.m. ET call](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/07/3376580/0/en/accel-entertainment-inc-to-report-third-quarter-2026-results-host-conference-call-and-webcast-on-november-3.html).
- **PENN Entertainment (PENN), Thursday Nov. 5, at 7:00 a.m. ET,** with a [9:00 a.m. call](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/PENN/penn-entertainment-to-report-third-quarter-results-and-host-1kzqwdtfnc79.html).

I could not confirm dates this morning for DraftKings (DKNG), Flutter (FLUT), MGM Resorts (MGM), Take-Two Interactive (TTWO) or others, so they are left out rather than guessed.

## Caesars: a report with no Q&A

The Caesars print is the one to read closely, mostly for what is missing. Under the [May 28 merger agreement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001590895/000119312526242995/d143382dex991.htm), Fertitta Entertainment would pay $31.00 in cash per share. The deal is valued at about $17.6 billion, including roughly $11.9 billion of assumed debt, and [Casino.org](https://casino.org/news/fertitta-entertainment-acquires-caesars-entertainment) calls it the largest U.S. casino acquisition on record. The price is a 49% premium to the unaffected share price in late February.

For a stock whose price is now anchored by a cash offer, the quarterly numbers matter less to the share price than to the deal's risk profile. Investors will look at the filing for operating trends in Las Vegas and regional markets, digital results and leverage, because those shape how comfortable lenders and regulators are.

Timing is the open question. According to the [filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001590895/000119312526242998/d143382ddefa14a.htm), closing needs shareholder approval, expiration of the HSR waiting period and gaming regulatory approvals. No shareholder meeting date has been set, and no closing date is given. The outside date is May 27, 2027, extendable in stages to Nov. 27, 2027, and a ticking fee applies if closing has not occurred by June 26, 2027. The go-shop window ended July 11. Any news on a proxy filing or approvals is worth more than the earnings line items.

## What the other reports should clarify

**PENN** is the first of these to report before the open, at 7:00 a.m. ET on Nov. 5, and the focus for investors is its online casino and sports betting business, where it competes with the digital specialists.

**BetMGM**, the joint venture between MGM Resorts (MGM) and Entain, offers a read on the digital side before the full reports. Its [Q2 update](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/betmgm-q2-2026-business-update-302835789.html) showed net revenue of $711 million, up 3%, with iGaming up 8% to $483 million and online sports flat at $228 million. Adjusted EBITDA fell 15% to $74 million, and management guided to the lower end of its $300 million to $350 million full-year range. It also said its $500 million long-term EBITDA target would likely slip beyond 2027. Anyone modeling the Q3 prints will want to see whether sports betting growth has stayed flat and whether iGaming is still carrying the segment.

**GLPI and Accel** are different animals. GLPI is a casino landlord whose results are driven by rent escalators and tenant health rather than gaming volume, so it tells you about operator solvency. Accel runs video gaming terminals in places like Illinois and offers a view of local, route-based gambling that national reports miss.

## Video games: the date that overrides earnings

For game publishers, the number that matters is Nov. 19. Take-Two said on its [July 28 report](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/112875/take-two-doubles-down-on-gta-6-release-date-expects-net-bookings-to-increase-by-22-percent/index.html) that Grand Theft Auto VI remains planned for that date, and guided fiscal 2027 net bookings to $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion, up roughly 19% to 22% from $6.72 billion. Because the launch sits inside the December quarter, the next report will be dominated by marketing signals and any change to that date.

Demand on PC looks healthy meanwhile. Alinea Analytics estimates Steam revenue of about $1.7 billion in September, up 13% year over year, and a record third quarter of about $5.5 billion, according to a [roundup of the data](https://shattered.io/steam-1-7-billion-september-record-2026). Valve does not publish financials, so these are third-party estimates, and the same roundup notes they are not directly comparable to console company disclosures.

## What to watch

- Whether Caesars files its proxy and sets a shareholder meeting date, and any word on regulatory approvals.
- Whether a missing earnings call changes how much operating detail Caesars gives in its release.
- BetMGM-type signals of flat sportsbook growth appearing in the PENN and operator reports.
- Confirmation of reporting dates for DraftKings, Flutter, MGM and Take-Two as they are announced.
- Any change to the Nov. 19 GTA VI date, the single largest swing factor for the sector's holiday quarter.
