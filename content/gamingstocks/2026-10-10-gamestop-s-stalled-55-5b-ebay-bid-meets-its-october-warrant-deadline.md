## Two very different paths to a mega-deal

Gaming's two largest pieces of dealmaking this year could hardly look more different. Electronic Arts is already done: its [roughly $55 billion take-private](https://engadget.com/2227680/electronic-arts-goes-private-next-week), billed as the largest leveraged buyout ever, was set to close on or about August 4, after all regulatory approvals were in hand on July 30. The buyers are Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, Silver Lake and Affinity Partners, and CEO Andrew Wilson stays on.

GameStop (GME) is the opposite case. Its [non-binding proposal for eBay (EBAY)](https://investor.gamestop.com/news-releases/news-details/2026/GameStop-Proposes-to-Acquire-eBay-at-125-00-Per-Share/), announced May 3, is five months old, rejected by the target, and still unresolved. With GameStop's warrants about to expire, it is the deal most likely to move a listed gaming stock in the next three weeks.

## What GameStop offered

The proposal was $125 per eBay share, split 50% cash and 50% GameStop stock, valuing eBay's undiluted equity at about $55.5 billion. GameStop said that was a 46% premium to eBay's unaffected close on February 4, the day it began building its stake. The cash was to come from GameStop's balance sheet, which held about $9.4 billion in cash and liquid investments at January 31, plus third-party financing. TD Securities provided a "highly-confident letter" for up to $20 billion.

That financing math is the heart of the dispute. [Barchart's account](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/gamestop-just-raised-stake-ebay-205407860.html) says eBay's board called the offer "neither credible nor attractive" and questioned how a much smaller company could pay for it, noting eBay's market value was almost four times GameStop's. The same piece reports that Chief Executive Ryan Cohen committed about $500 million of his own money, dropped a proposed CEO award reportedly worth up to $35 billion, and said he would go directly to shareholders if the board kept refusing.

## What has happened since

GameStop did not walk away. A Barchart report cites an SEC filing showing GameStop raised its eBay position to nearly 10%, about 43.4 million shares. [Bloomberg reported on August 10](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/gamestop-ryan-cohen-weighs-pulling-114718396.html), citing people familiar with the matter, that Cohen was weighing withdrawing the bid and instead pitching a partnership or joint venture that would let eBay use GameStop's roughly 1,600 U.S. stores to grow in trading cards and collectibles. Under any such arrangement GameStop would seek a board seat. Bloomberg stressed no final decision had been made.

The market's verdict on the bid, at least as of that report, was lopsided. GameStop's shares were down 28% since the offer while eBay's were up 7.6%. GameStop's market value had slipped to $8.6 billion, close to its $8.4 billion in cash. Michael Burry's Scion Asset Management, per the same report, sold its entire GameStop position after the bid, citing the debt GameStop could take on.

I found no announced withdrawal or revised terms in the sources I reviewed. As of [October 8 coverage](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/10/08/gamestop-rises-4-but-stays-far-below-its-warrant-strike-take-two-interactive-gains-3-roblox-falls-3), the same outlet said GameStop had not disclosed a fresh development tied to the latest move in its stock.

## The warrant clock

The near-term catalyst is not regulatory. GameStop's warrants carry a $32 exercise price and expire at the end of October. On October 8, GME traded around $25.47, up 4% on the day and 33% over the past month, still roughly $7 below the strike. The article also notes the company's large cash position gives it options beyond the eBay proposal, and that Cohen has been buying shares in the open market with his own money.

For investors, the mechanics matter more than the narrative. If shares finish October below $32, the warrants lapse unexercised and GameStop receives no exercise cash. If they finish above, exercising would bring in cash and add shares, diluting holders. Either outcome interacts with any eventual eBay offer, because half the consideration in the original proposal is GameStop stock whose value has moved sharply since May.

## The rest of the deal market

Outside the headline pursuit, activity is steadier. Drake Star's [Q2 2026 report](https://www.drakestar.com/news/global-gaming-report-q2-2026) counted 51 announced gaming M&A deals, mostly smaller and mid-sized platform and tools companies and game studios, alongside more than $2.5 billion raised in 96 private financing rounds. Public-market issuance included a $502 million IPO for Liftoff Mobile.

In betting, Track360's tracker records Genius Sports (GENI) completing its acquisition of Legend, the owner of Covers.com, Casino.org and Casino Guru, on May 1 at $900 million at close ($800 million cash and $100 million stock), plus an earn-out of up to $300 million. Evolution's roughly $85 million purchase of Galaxy Gaming was still listed as pending in that tracker's July snapshot, so its status needs checking against company filings.

The pattern is consolidation by well-funded owners and strategic buyers of assets with clear revenue, as with EA and Legend, while the outsized, stock-funded reach from GameStop has met resistance.

## What to watch

- **GME against $32.** Whether the shares approach the warrant strike before month-end, and whether GameStop files anything on its eBay stake.
- **Any eBay response.** A revised proposal, a partnership announcement or a formal withdrawal would each reprice both stocks.
- **Financing signals.** The TD letter covers up to $20 billion, while the original package implied far more cash than GameStop holds.
- **Sector read-through.** Take-Two (TTWO) rose 3% to $210.50 on October 8 on anticipation of GTA VI, a reminder that most large-cap gaming stocks are still trading on product cycles rather than deals.

None of this is a recommendation. It is a snapshot of where the sector's biggest transactions stand, and one of them is still without an ending.
