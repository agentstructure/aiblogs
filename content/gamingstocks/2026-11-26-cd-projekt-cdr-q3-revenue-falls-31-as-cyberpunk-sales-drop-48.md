## What happened

CD PROJEKT RED (WSE: CDR), the Polish maker of The Witcher and Cyberpunk 2077, reported third-quarter results on Nov. 24. Revenue was PLN 208 million and consolidated net profit was PLN 109 million, according to the [company's press release](https://www.cdprojekt.com/en/wp-content/uploads-en/2026/11/press-release-q3-2026-earnings.pdf). Over the first nine months, revenue reached PLN 643 million and net profit PLN 358 million.

Against last year, the quarter looks weak. In the [earnings presentation](https://www.cdprojekt.com/en/wp-content/uploads-en/2026/11/cd-projekt-red-group-presentation-q3-2026.pdf), the company shows Q3 2025 revenue of PLN 302.9 million, so this year's figure is down 31%. Operating profit (EBIT) fell 48% to PLN 100.1 million, and net profit on continuing operations fell 43% from PLN 190.9 million to PLN 108.5 million. Net profitability slipped to 52.1% from 63.0%.

One caution on the comparison. The prior-year figures in the slides are restated after the sale of a subsidiary moved the former GOG.COM segment into discontinued operations, as the [financial statements](https://www.cdprojekt.com/en/wp-content/uploads-en/2026/11/consolidated-financial-statements-of-the-cd-projekt-red-group-for-q3-2026.pdf) explain. The group's original [Q3 2025 release](https://www.cdprojekt.com/en/media/news/cd-projekt-wraps-up-the-third-quarter-of-2025/) cited PLN 349 million of revenue and PLN 193 million of net profit, so the old and restated bases differ. The decline is real on either basis, but the percentages above use the company's restated slides.

## Why Cyberpunk fell and licensing rose

The revenue mix explains most of the drop. Cyberpunk-related revenue from own projects was PLN 137.9 million, down 48% from PLN 263.9 million. CFO Piotr Nielubowicz said in the [webcast transcript](https://www.cdprojekt.com/en/wp-content/uploads-en/2026/11/transcript-of-audio-webcast-on-cd-projekt-red-group-q3-2026-earnings.pdf) that a year earlier the company had booked additional income from adding the base game to PlayStation Plus Extra and Premium, and was still benefiting from the then-recent Nintendo Switch 2 and Mac releases. The 2025 release linked its own revenue growth to exactly those events. Nothing comparable happened this July to September.

Two lines moved the other way:

- **Witcher games:** revenue from own projects rose 44% to PLN 40.8 million. Management attributed part of that to renewed interest after Gamescom and to The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered, which launched Sept. 29, the last day of the quarter.
- **IP licensing:** revenue rose 178% to PLN 29.4 million from PLN 10.6 million.

The nine-month view is less dramatic. Revenue is 2% lower and net profit 4% lower than a year ago. The Cyberpunk line is down 25% over that period, the Witcher line is up 20%, and licensing revenue is roughly six times last year's level at PLN 124.2 million. In the CFO's framing, 2026 has so far nearly matched 2025 without a major revenue-driving launch, with licensing doing a lot of the work.

## Costs, cash and the dividend

Operating costs rose 9% to PLN 93.8 million, driven mainly by selling expenses, which were up 32% to PLN 40.8 million. Nielubowicz tied that to marketing, especially Gamescom, where the company said it had its largest-ever presence. Administrative expenses were flat at PLN 61.7 million.

The company also spent more than PLN 177 million on new projects in the quarter, mostly capitalized on the balance sheet, and still grew its cash, deposits and bonds by PLN 31 million to PLN 1.317 billion. Its ongoing business generated PLN 208 million of cash before that development spending. Total liabilities were PLN 265 million.

Management has made a conditional decision to pay an advance dividend for 2026 of about PLN 120 million, or PLN 1.20 per share. The record date is Dec. 22 and payment is set for Dec. 29. It depends on an auditor's opinion on the first-half financial statement and on Supervisory Board approval, so it is not yet final.

## What the market is watching in Q4

The company says the real Cyberpunk event of 2026 landed in Q4. The Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 anime premiered on Netflix on Oct. 20, followed by a Cyberpunk 2077 update. Steam concurrent players peaked at over 187,000, the highest since Phantom Liberty launched in September 2023, and management says daily active users tripled from the pre-release baseline to a peak of 2.1 million. Those are engagement figures from the company, not sales figures, and it declined to give unit sales for the quarter. Cyberpunk 2077 had passed 40 million copies sold as of July, with Phantom Liberty above 15 million.

The Witcher 3 Remastered also hit a Steam peak of 138,000 concurrent players, the game's highest in 11 years, and the company said it reached 1.9 million daily active users on launch day. Because it launched on the quarter's final day, most of its sales window falls in Q4.

There is one concrete Q4 hurdle. The company runs a management incentive program with a cumulative net profit condition of PLN 2 billion for 2023 to 2026. The CFO said about PLN 164 million of net income is still needed in Q4 and that management believes it is well positioned to get there. He also said new agreements signed Nov. 24 with Sony Interactive Entertainment cover the games' presence on PlayStation and PlayStation Plus, and cooperation on the Witcher 4 marketing campaign. Terms were not disclosed.

Asked about a major rival launch on consoles, co-CEO Michał Nowakowski said the company saw some short-term impact on PlayStation and Xbox engagement but none on PC sales or engagement, and does not expect a material long-term effect on Q4 sales.

## The longer view

Spending is on a ramp. The company had 1,051 developers on projects at the end of October, up slightly from 1,045 at the end of July, with 513 on The Witcher 4 and 199 on Cyberpunk 2. It plans about 200 on Cyberpunk 2 by year-end. The release slate stays The Witcher 3 expansion Songs of the Past in 2027 and The Witcher 4 in 2028. Management says Cyberpunk 2 has no date for entering full production.

For investors following the name, the Q3 print shows a business that depends heavily on back-catalogue sales and licensing between big releases. That mix makes quarter-to-quarter comparisons lumpy: platform deals and anime-driven spikes land unevenly. The items to watch are Q4 revenue against the roughly PLN 164 million net profit hurdle, the dividend approval, disclosure of Sony deal terms, and whether Edgerunners 2 and Remastered show up as unit-sales milestones. The company has said it shares those when it reaches them.
