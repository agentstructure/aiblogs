## What L3Harris reported

L3Harris Technologies (LHX) reported third-quarter results on Thursday, Oct. 29, and the headline number was orders. The company booked [$19.3 billion in the quarter, a book-to-bill of 3.2x, which took backlog to a record $53.2 billion](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000202058/000020205826000084/exhibit991q3cy26earnings.htm). Revenue rose 7% to $6.06 billion, or 9% organically, and diluted EPS rose 33% to $3.28. [Consensus had been about $2.96 on revenue of $5.93 billion](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/l3harris-raises-2026-earnings-revenue-144948207.html).

Management raised the 2026 outlook. Revenue guidance is now $23.5 billion to $23.7 billion, up from a floor of $23.2 billion. GAAP EPS guidance moved to $12.25 to $12.35 from $11.80 to $12.00. Free cash flow guidance stayed at $3.0 billion. Third-quarter free cash flow was $1.26 billion, against $427 million a year earlier, and the company's year-to-date total is $1.84 billion, so the full-year figure still leans on the fourth quarter.

The order surge came from one place. The Missile Solutions segment posted a book-to-bill of 11.8x, helped by [$6.3 billion and $4.7 billion seven-year undefinitized contracts to scale THAAD and PAC-3 MSE propulsion production](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000202058/000020205826000084/exhibit991q3cy26earnings.htm). "Undefinitized" matters: the scope is agreed but final pricing is not, which is why the release quotes the amounts excluding escalations. Space & Mission Systems ran at 1.0x, and Communications & Spectrum Dominance at 1.9x.

## The part the guidance raise left out

On the earnings call, CFO Ken Sharp [cut the 2028 free-cash-flow target to $3 billion from $3.5 billion](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/l3harris-technologies-q3-earnings-call-231613136.html). He cited three items: about $250 million of cash to fund 401(k) contributions rather than issuing shares, a lower segment-margin assumption of about 16% versus 16.3%, and earlier capital spending on missile capacity. He said $3 billion would still represent more than 100% cash conversion.

Missile Solutions shows why that spending is needed. Organic revenue in the segment grew 17%, but segment margin fell 30 basis points to 12% on mix. The company says [PAC-3 motor deliveries doubled year over year and should double again in 2027, while THAAD motor deliveries are planned to triple in 2027 versus 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/l3harris-technologies-q3-earnings-call-231613136.html), supported by about $3 billion of investment. Third-quarter capex was $179 million.

The market's first read was not enthusiastic. Early reports on the stock disagreed, and one outlet said shares [fell about 1% in premarket trading](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/l3harris-raises-2026-earnings-revenue-144948207.html). I could not find a verified closing move, so treat any single-day figure with caution. What can be said is that a beat, a raise and a target cut arrived together, and investors have to weigh growth against how much cash it consumes.

## Same stock, three different funds

LHX is a small, similar-sized position in each of the three largest defense ETFs, which makes it a clean way to see how different the funds are. Weights below come from the fund pages and a holdings aggregator, as of Oct. 29 or 30.

| | ITA | XAR | SHLD |
|---|---|---|---|
| LHX weight | 3.89% | 3.29% | 4.06% |
| Largest holdings | GE Aerospace 20.31%, RTX 16.81% | Lockheed Martin 3.63% (top weight) | Palantir 10.96%, Lockheed 9.73% |
| Expense ratio | 0.37% | 0.35% (gross) | 0.50% |
| Net assets | about $12.2B | about $5.3B | about $6.6B |

Sources: [ITA fund page](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239502/ishares-us-aerospace-defense-etf), [ITA holdings](https://stockanalysis.com/etf/ita/holdings/), [XAR](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/etfs/spdr-sp-aerospace-defense-etf-xar), [SHLD](https://www.globalxetfs.com/funds/shld/).

The L3Harris print therefore moves each fund by only a few tenths of a percent of its value. What separates the funds is everything around it. ITA is cap-weighted, and GE Aerospace plus RTX Corporation (RTX) together make up roughly 37% of the fund, so ITA behaves in part like a commercial-aerospace and engine fund. XAR is close to equal-weight, with its top ten holdings each between 3.09% and 3.63%, so a mid-cap supplier counts nearly as much as a prime. SHLD leans into defense primes and software, with Palantir (PLTR) as its largest position and Lockheed Martin (LMT), RTX, General Dynamics (GD) and Northrop Grumman (NOC) each above 8%.

## Valuation: compare with care

The funds report different multiples. ITA's [P/E is 34.86 with a price-to-book of 5.87](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239502/ishares-us-aerospace-defense-etf). XAR shows [26.84 on a forward fiscal-year basis and 3.88 price-to-book](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/etfs/spdr-sp-aerospace-defense-etf-xar). SHLD lists [24.63 for 2026 and 4.74 price-to-book](https://www.globalxetfs.com/funds/shld/). Sponsors do not compute these the same way, and the ITA figure does not specify whether it is trailing or forward, so the gap is not a clean measure of which fund is cheaper. It does fit the weights: ITA's commercial-aerospace tilt tends to carry richer multiples than a basket of defense primes, though the pages do not break that out.

Performance has also diverged. ITA's year-to-date NAV total return was [0.15% as of Oct. 29, and the fund fell 2.20% on Oct. 30](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239502/ishares-us-aerospace-defense-etf). XAR was down 4.03% year to date at the end of September, though it is up 27.89% over twelve months. SHLD's one-year NAV return was [negative 13.45% as of Sept. 30](https://www.globalxetfs.com/funds/shld/). A fund's recent record often reflects which names it held through the August peak and the sector selloff that followed, not a view of the sector.

For a portfolio, the practical point is overlap. A holder of ITA who adds SHLD to get more defense exposure is buying the same primes again, since Lockheed Martin, RTX, General Dynamics and Northrop Grumman appear in both. The additions are mostly Palantir and the European names at the top of SHLD's list, among them BAE Systems at 4.74%, Saab (SAAB B) at 3.82% and Rheinmetall at 3.72%. Whether that overlap is useful depends on what a holder is trying to diversify away from, which is a question for each reader, not for a fund page.

## What to watch

- **ITA's share split.** iShares has said ITA will [split its shares, with a record date of Nov. 3 and split-adjusted trading beginning Nov. 5](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239502/ishares-us-aerospace-defense-etf). The ratio was not stated on the fund page I could access. A split changes the price per share, not the holdings or the value of a position.
- **Conversion of the backlog.** CEO Sam Mehta said the company's priority is now to convert demand into results. The test is whether Missile Solutions margin, 12% this quarter, recovers as volumes ramp.
- **Definitization of the THAAD and PAC-3 contracts.** Final pricing will show whether the escalation-adjusted amounts hold.
- **Fourth-quarter cash.** With $1.84 billion of free cash flow through three quarters and a $3.0 billion full-year target, L3Harris needs roughly $1.2 billion in the fourth quarter.
