## The week has a second layer

Most of the attention this week goes to Microsoft (MSFT), Alphabet (GOOGL) and Amazon (AMZN), and for good reason. [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-oct-26-2026) notes that investors are focused on capital expenditure guidance from those three, and [HeyGoTrade](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/news/weekly-economic-outlook-2026-10-26/) puts expected hyperscaler capital spending at around $800 billion this year. That story has had plenty of coverage here already.

The rest of the calendar gets less airtime, and it matters more to anyone watching a base that is not a megacap. Over four sessions, Boeing (BA), Seagate (STX), Sandisk (SNDK), Robinhood (HOOD), Exxon Mobil (XOM) and Chevron (CVX) all report, along with Eli Lilly (LLY) and many others. Each one sets a different kind of test for a stock that has been tightening up.

One caution before the numbers. Consensus estimates differ by source. [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/earnings-calendar-week-of-october-26-2026), which cites Nasdaq data, and [Kiplinger](https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/stocks/17494/next-week-earnings-calendar-stocks) disagree on several. Treat every figure below as approximate, and note that the dates for Corning, PayPal and Ford were still marked as expected, not confirmed, on StockTitan's list.

## Tuesday: Boeing and the turnaround test

Boeing reports before the open on Tuesday. StockTitan lists consensus at a loss of $0.21 a share against a loss of $7.47 a year ago. Kiplinger shows a loss of $0.25. The gap between the two sources is small, but the point is that a turnaround story is being judged on how much smaller the loss is, not on a profit.

For a trader, that framing is useful. A stock that has built a base on improving losses is already pricing in some improvement. The question the report answers is whether the improvement is arriving faster or slower than that. A gap up on a loss that narrows more than expected is a different animal from a gap up into a prior high on thin volume.

Robinhood reports after the close the same day. The consensus is $0.56 a share, down from $0.61 a year ago on StockTitan's list. Seagate and NXP Semiconductors (NXPI) also report Tuesday after the close.

## Thursday: Sandisk and a very large year-over-year jump

The most striking number on the calendar belongs to Sandisk. StockTitan shows a consensus of $45.22 a share for the report due Thursday after the close, against $0.90 a year ago. Seagate carries a similar shape two days earlier: $7.17 expected against $2.51 a year ago.

Those are storage and memory names, and the size of the comparison tells you the expectations are already enormous. That is the setup risk. When a year-over-year comparison is that lopsided, the stock usually has a lot of good news baked in, and the reaction depends on guidance and on how the print compares with whisper numbers that no calendar publishes.

Eli Lilly reports Thursday before the open at $5.38 a share on StockTitan's list, below the $7.02 it earned a year ago. Kiplinger has $5.70. Lilly's own 2026 revenue range, per StockTitan, is $85.0 billion to $87.0 billion. Intel (INTC) and Amazon report after the close, and Reddit (RDDT) and Rivian (RIVN) are also confirmed for Thursday after the close.

Thursday is also the busiest day of the week, with 203 reports, according to StockTitan. It is also the day of the advance third-quarter GDP reading, which [HeyGoTrade](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/news/weekly-economic-outlook-2026-10-26/) says is forecast at around 3%, along with the monthly core PCE reading, forecast at 0.3%. Crowding that many catalysts into a single session tends to make intraday ranges wide and the first move unreliable.

## Friday: Exxon and Chevron against $100 oil

Exxon and Chevron both report Friday before the open. StockTitan has consensus at $3.96 for Exxon against $1.88 a year ago, and $4.78 for Chevron against $1.85. Kiplinger's figures are higher, at $4.10 and $4.86. By either measure, the estimate is more than double last year's.

The backdrop is crude. [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-oct-26-2026) reports West Texas Intermediate at $101.40 a barrel and Brent at $115.20 on Monday, both lower on hopes of easing US-Iran tensions. So the earnings come with the commodity price already high and moving on geopolitical headlines.

That creates a specific problem for energy bases. A strong quarter is expected, so the print itself is less informative than the commentary on capital spending and production. And the stock can react to the headline price of oil the same morning, independent of anything in the report. Phillips 66 (PSX), which reports Wednesday before the open, gives an early read: StockTitan lists consensus at $12.02 against $2.52 a year ago.

## The macro frame

The Federal Reserve decides Wednesday. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/expect-markets-week-googl-msft-152700674.html) cites CME FedWatch at 90% odds of no change, following a hike in September, and HeyGoTrade cites futures pricing around an 80% chance of another hike in December. On Friday the Employment Cost Index and Chicago PMI also land.

The same source reports that S&P 500 third-quarter earnings growth is running at about 29.5% year over year per FactSet, with 24% of companies reported as of October 23. A bar that high means in-line results can read as a miss.

## What to watch

A few practical checks apply to any stock you are following, whatever its sector.

- **Volume on the reaction day.** A move through a pivot on volume well above the recent average carries more weight than the same move on a quiet tape.
- **Where it closes, not where it opens.** Several of these reports come before the open, and a morning gap that fades by the close says something different from one that holds.
- **Distance from the base.** A stock already extended well above its pivot going into a print has less room than one still sitting in the base.
- **Neighbors.** A strong Seagate print on Tuesday can pull Sandisk and other storage names before they report. A weak one can do the opposite.

None of this predicts direction. It is a way to read what happens once the numbers are out. The calendar tells you when the tests arrive. The tape, on the day, tells you whether a base passed.
